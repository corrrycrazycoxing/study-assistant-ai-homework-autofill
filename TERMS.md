# Study Assistant terms of use

**Last updated:** October 2, 2026

Study Assistant is an independent educational review tool. By installing or using it, you agree to use it only where you are authorized to do so and to follow your school's, instructor's, employer's, and learning platform's rules.

## What the extension does

The extension extracts supported question data, sends a user-requested prompt to the selected AI service, shows a reviewable answer, and can enter declarative answers into reviewed controls. AI output may be incomplete or wrong. The user must review each answer and remains responsible for every submission, attempt, grade, and course-policy decision.

The extension does not promise support for every site layout or control. It does not bypass access controls, defeat anti-cheat systems, spoof a browser fingerprint, forge trusted events, or submit a final assignment on the user's behalf.

## Third-party services

The extension can interact with third-party learning sites and AI services selected by the user. Their availability, terms, privacy practices, content, and decisions are outside the developer's control. A user must review those providers' terms before using this extension with their content.

## Acceptable use

Do not use the extension to access data or assignments without authorization, evade a school's assessment rules, impersonate another person, interfere with a learning platform, or submit work that violates an instructor's requirements. Stop the run and enter answers manually when the extension reports an unsupported or changed question.

## Availability and changes

The extension is provided as an evolving local project. Features may change, stop working when a provider changes its page, or require a new version. Chrome may install store updates automatically after review; an update notice may appear in the extension panel when Chrome has downloaded one.

To the extent permitted by law, the developer makes no promise that it will be uninterrupted, error-free, compatible with every assignment, or suitable for a particular grade or purpose.
