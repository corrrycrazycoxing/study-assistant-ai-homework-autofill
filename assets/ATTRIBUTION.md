# Provider icons

These locally bundled images identify the selectable services. Study Assistant is independent and is not endorsed by OpenAI, Google or DeepSeek. Trademarks remain their owners’ property and are not licensed by this extension’s MIT license.

- ChatGPT: unmodified white OpenAI Blossom from the official OpenAI Agents SDK repository, rasterized from SVG to PNG without recoloring: https://github.com/openai/openai-agents-python/blob/main/docs/assets/logo.svg . Branding guidance: https://openai.com/brand/ .
- Gemini: public official Google PNG, retrieved from the Gemini homepage: https://www.gstatic.com/lamda/images/gemini_sparkle_4g_512_lt_f94943af3be039176192d.png .
- DeepSeek: official public favicon https://deepseek.com/favicon.ico, converted to PNG without changing its pixels.

Public distribution requires a separate review of all applicable brand and service terms. These are service identifiers, not the extension’s own branding.
