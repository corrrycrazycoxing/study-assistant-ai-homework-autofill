READINGS & SOURCES

Why use NotebookLM instead of only a chatbot?

Reading-based questions may expect your course’s specific definitions, examples or author’s wording. NotebookLM uses the sources you select and can cite passages to check. A general chatbot may answer from broader knowledge that does not match those readings.

Add your assigned readings to a notebook and select the relevant sources. This extension gets the source-based answer there, then a regular chatbot formats it for input. You still need to verify the answer and citations. With no notebook open it falls back to a chatbot; if an open notebook’s sources do not support an answer, the run stops for review.
